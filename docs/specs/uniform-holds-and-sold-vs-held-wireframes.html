Spec: uniform-holds-and-sold-vs-held.md. Desktop and phone for each admin or organizer surface, with default, loading, empty and error states where they apply. Mock screens use the product's light palette; the navbar and UAT banner stay on every screen. Dates always read Sat, Oct 3, 2026 · 9:30 AM in Philippine time.   • Meaning, in words not colour: every chip carries its label. Sold = approved registrations and valid tickets. Awaiting approval = proof uploaded, not yet approved. Unpaid holds = checkout started, no proof yet. Zero parts are left out; if everything is zero the row says "0 sold".
  • The chips are text, ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, and the row remains readable at 320 px by wrapping. "View unpaid checkouts" opens Transactions filtered to that event and status "pending" (existing filter).
  • Each chip has a title/long-press explanation (for example "Seats held by checkouts that have not uploaded payment proof yet. They are released automatically after about an hour.").
  • Public pages keep showing only "N slots left"; none of these numbers appear there.
