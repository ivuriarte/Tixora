While the page checks (existing skeleton, role="status")

Checking your registration…

If the check fails for another reason (network), the normal form is shown and the save step is the safety net.
  • Shown the moment a signed-in visitor opens a registration that isn't theirs, before any form, and also if the save is refused. No request is repeated.
  • The hidden page heading is "Your registration" on the payment page. It never shows another person's details. No sentence on this screen uses "sign-in", "order" or an email address.
  • For a customer who already paid, Contact is the main action and Start again is the quieter outlined button, so they are not drawn to pay twice.
  • The 16 px text applies to this screen only. Other end screens keep their 14 px text. At 320 px the text column is narrow, so the buttons sit below the first screen; the build measures the fold at 320x568.
